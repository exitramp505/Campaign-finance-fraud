⚖️ Why These Donations Are Illegal

Federal Law (Johnson Amendment)
501(c)(3) organizations, churches and nonprofits like IMA of Toledo, are prohibited from making direct contributions to political campaigns.
Ohio Law
Campaigns cannot accept money from churches or nonprofits. These rules prevent tax-subsidized groups from funding candidates.

⚖️ Why This Is Unfair

Democrats often demand that churches “stay out of politics,” especially conservative ones. Yet when leftist churches and 501(c)(3) nonprofits quietly fund Democratic candidates like McPherson, the same critics say nothing. This double standard undermines the very rules meant to keep tax-exempt groups out of elections.
